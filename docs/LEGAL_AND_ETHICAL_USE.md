# Legal and Ethical Use

Use this project only on systems and networks you own or where you have explicit, documented authorization. Confirm scope, time window, permitted techniques, and contacts before scanning.

Private IP ranges are not a permission mechanism. Software restrictions are technical guardrails; they do not grant legal authority. Do not scan public Internet targets, third-party systems, school/work networks, or shared infrastructure without written approval.

This educational tool is not a penetration-testing certification, vulnerability scanner, or guarantee of security. Follow applicable laws, contracts, organizational policy, and responsible disclosure practices.
